Hello, I am Lester. I build production apps. But I wasn't until ten years ago. Let's back up a bit.

## Education

I took up Bachelor of Science in Agricultural Economics major in Agricultural Marketing and Prices at the University of the Philippines - Los Baños and graduated Cum laude in 2011. In 2016, I started taking Master in Development Economics at the University of the Philippines - Diliman and completed it in 2021. 

While studying in masters, I took up web development on the side. My colleague shared a book with me about learning PHP and MySQL in 24 hours. Yes, that kind of book. I started it as a pastime and a hobby to cure the boredom during weekends. I started building the sample app in there, an e-commerce app. I had fun so I decided, I'd build an actual app for my work. I'll talk about it later. Later on, I explored even more tech stacks like Laravel, Vuejs, React, Quasar Framework, Python, Dart, Flutter, and so on, every time I need to solve a problem. I bought courses in Udemy as well to further my knowledge. I joined several communities in Facebook and Reddit so I could interact with others. I'd find question and try to answer them. What better way to learn than helping others learn? In this way, I learned a lot in software development. The process never ends, I learn something new every day.

## Work Experience

### Government

I worked a total of 12 years in the government at the Department of Agriculture (DA) Central Office. My first role was to gather data and process them for the Secretary and his advisers' consumption. All data in agriculture and fisheries sector and some general data like population, land area, etc. All the boring stuff. It was fun though. I used pivot table mainly. My day consisted of encoding data, researching, downloading, putting them together in Excel files, organizing data, etc. Like I said all those boring stuff. Then, my boss started getting me involved in reviewing and writing reports. Then, go to meetings, get exposed myself to inner workings of the government. BORING. But anyway, fast forward to the exciting stuff.

What really got me to web development was when NEDA (the National Economic and Development Authority) introduced this web-based system called "PIPOL" or the Public Investment Program Online System. It was built using PHP (of course, I didn't know it back then). In our office, we've been using Google Sheets to gather data for our work to consolidate and process the entries we will input into the PIPOL System. We coordinated with over 50 offices, got their input, we reviewed them and added comments on each google sheets. Whew, what an excruciating process. Then, we do it the following year. Review the same and so on. Same process. This is when the idea came to me, why not just build our own? Welp, that didn't happen for the next three years. LOL. Instead the first actual system I built was the AFMP-DPS (it's a fancy acronym for Agriculture and Fisheries Modernization Plan - Data Processing System). It's a web-based system I built and hosted using my own money (yeah, government don't provide for personal projects even for official use). It was built using PHP, Semantic UI, JQuery, and MySQL. The key thing I had to learn here was that data was coming from a 3rd party, SurveyCTO. So users input there and I just sync the database using REST API. Then, they make changes using the system I built. It was awesome but unfortunately the system was one-time use because it was built for that project. It was good experience though.

Stuck at home during the pandemic, I worked on our home-grown PIPOL System. Back then we called it the Investment Programming Management System (IPMS) in 2020. I've been playing around with Laravel and Quasar Framework back then. The scope was PIPOL plus our own requirements. It was the first prototype. Then, finally in 2021, I built the Public Investment Program System (PIPS). It had the same feature as PIPOL with some additional features for commenting and review. In 2021, I left the agency to pursue my own career in software development. In 2022, I worked for a month and a half in the LTFRB (Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board) where I was task to build RideMap (a mobile app for ridership monitoring under their Libreng Sakay Program). I finished the prototype built with Laravel and React Native and handed it off to the team. In 2022, I went back to the DA to rebuild and improve the PIPS. I built it with Laravel backend but this time, the frontend is Flutter but for web only. After the successful rebuild, I left the agency for good to pursue career in freelancing.

### Freelancing

My freelancing started before I left the government in December 2023. I started first with people I could find in Facebook. Students reaching out to build their capstone (it's bad, but every project is a learning opportunity). So I worked on Flutter app for medicine reminder, I assisted a graduating student on a React project build their React web app for maintaining clinic schedule, a simple web app to gather and process some data, and so on. I also built some apps with accompanying tutorial for an Upwork client and another one is for a Flutter mobile apps for skating rink music cuing. There's another project offered to me to build a prototype but the client ghosted me (ouch).

Eventually, I found my way to Tellycom LLC and has been building their startup apps using Flutter together with a solid team of freelancers. So far, I like where I am but I want to explore more and learn more.
